## Colors (TTY only; piped output and `NO_COLOR` stay plain)
- The art uses the three palette entries from `assets/logo.json`: sky `#7fd8ff`, orange `#f66047`, navy `#027bb0`, per cell, in truecolor; the 256-color fallback maps sky→117, orange→203, navy→31. ## Layout, 80 columns, compact logo (13 rows) left, text right starting three spaces after the widest art row

A new user has no positions, so the greeting leads with the market, live. A full venue scan measures 4.5 s today (`mob scan --top 3 --json` on 2026-10-13), so the greeting runs the real scan with a five-second budget rather than relying on a cache.

```text
                  #-------------   mstack 0.4.0 · 0xA4e8…fBde
                ###-------------   ───────────────────────────
              #####-------------   Top pairs  RSR    long hl · short bybit       837% net   $5.4M
      +++++++-#####-------------              KAIA   long binance · short bybit  236% net   $6.5M
    +++++++++######-------------              ZHONGJI long binance · short okx    213% net   $6.6M
  +++++++++++######-------------   Trending   BTC    $31.2B 24h · funding +9%/yr · OI $4.1B
 ++++++++++++######-------------              ETH    $18.9B 24h · funding +12%/yr · OI $2.2B
 -------------######+++++++++++               SOL    $4.3B 24h · funding −48%/yr · OI $0.9B
  ------------######+++++++++      Positions  1 running · $1,000 per leg · est. funding +$0.42/day
  ------------######+++++++        Ready      hl ✓  lighter ✓ · key mobius_ab12… · cap $5,000
  ------------#####                Policy     manual · you approve each Proposal by naming it
  ------------###                  Next       say "open RSR"   or   mob scan
  ------------#                    live · 7 of 7 venues · 4.5 s          ■■ sky  ■■ orange  ■■ navy
```

Rows, in order, and what each one is for:
- **Top pairs** (three rows): the best executable pairs by net APY from a live scan run by the greeting itself with a five-second budget: base, long and short venues, net APY, and the thinner leg's 24h volume. Venues that miss the budget are left out and named in the footer (`live · 5 of 7 venues · okx, aster timed out`). With no venue answering, show the cached scan with its age; with no cache either, one row `Top pairs  venues unreachable · mob scan to retry`.
- **Trending** (three rows): the markets with the highest 24h notional volume across the executable venues, from the same reads (the adapters already return 24h volume and open interest): base, 24h volume, current annualized funding with its sign, open interest. Add the 24h price change only where the venue's single call already carries it (Hyperliquid `prevDayPx`, the Binance, Bybit and OKX tickers); omit it otherwise rather than fetching more.
- **Positions**: only when the status read returns at least one task: `n running · $size per leg · est. funding +$x/day` (the estimate only when the API provides it). No row at all when the user has no positions or no key.
- **Ready**: bound venues `✓`, needs repair `✗`, the key prefix, its check and cap. Before onboarding the row is `Onboarding  legal ✗ · hl – · lighter – · key none` and Next says `say "set me up"`.
- **Policy**: `manual · you approve each Proposal by naming it`, `auto · the agent may submit inside the key's caps` in orange, or `locked · reads only`.
- **Next**: one or two actions derived from the same logic the onboarding checklist uses; after onboarding the first action names the top pair.
- **Footer**: freshness (`live · 7 of 7 venues · 4.5 s`, `cached 4 min ago`, or `offline`) on the left of the palette row; in ASCII mode the freshness alone.
Drop the Signer, Legal, API latency and full-Owner rows; `mob doctor` keeps them. `--offline` uses the cache only. Rows past the art continue below it. Under 72 columns, or with `--stacked`, fall back to the stacked layout. The whole block sits inside the `<<<MOB` and `MOB>>>` markers in ASCII mode. The preamble splash (first skill call of a session) runs the same live greeting with the same budget. `--json` gains `market: { topPairs, trending, freshness }`.
