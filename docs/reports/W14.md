## Decisions and limits

- Interruption takes precedence over expiry when a permanent marker exists without a signature. Rejection takes precedence over both. A spent marker never permits another signature; reject and stage a fresh Proposal. Cancellation before the attempt marker leaves the original Proposal signable until its original expiry.
- Dead pids and owners older than ten minutes are reclaimed. A legacy empty lock has a one-minute grace period, which also covers a crash between directory creation and owner write. A damaged owner record can be reclaimed after ten minutes. A live lock under the age limit is not stolen. `mob reject` closes interrupted/expired unsigned Proposals after stale-lock recovery; it does not pretend to undo a submitted signature.
- `slow` starts at three seconds. API reachability is advisory and does not gate scans or change selected venues, filters, ranking, or failure reporting.
- Hidden counts apply after market/APY filters and count thin pairs before cross-deployment pairs, avoiding double counting. `executable` remains an execution-support flag, not a readiness or profitability guarantee.
- The existing guard's literal-command authorization rules are unchanged. Its current signer allowlist remains browser/keystore; this package moves enforcement into the binary without broadening permissions.
- No live WalletConnect pairing, legal acceptance, binding, or trading smoke was attempted. Real wallet approval remains a human check. The existing local smokes and fixture tests cover these paths without a live write.
- W13/W16 areas are unchanged: no AGENTS.md, README, plugin metadata, hygiene files, release workflow, install script, shim, version, dependency, or lockfile edits. W14 can be rebased when those packages land; the generated preambles are mechanical and the only authored skill-template change is the glossary sentence in the three requested templates.

No open implementation question remains.
