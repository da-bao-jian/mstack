# W26 — seven more scan venues

Built on `main` after the 0.4.1 release. No runtime dependency was added. The version was not changed; the notes sit under Unreleased.

## Scope

Add MEXC, BingX, Bitget, Gate, GRVT, Variational, and QFEX to the funding scan with the same
rules as the existing adapters: public bulk feeds only, native market names, fail-fast
validation inside the adapter, recorded fixtures, pinned expected rows, and no live calls in
`bun test`.

## Sources

Each endpoint was requested once on 2026-10-18 and the full response hashed into
`packages/funding/fixtures/manifest.json`:

- MEXC `contract/detail`, bulk `contract/funding_rate` (rate, `collectCycle`, `nextSettleTime`,
  `fairPrice`), and `contract/ticker` (`amount24`, `holdVol`, `riseFallRate`).
- BingX `quote/premiumIndex` (rate, `fundingIntervalHours`, `nextFundingTime`, `markPrice`) and
  `quote/ticker` (`quoteVolume`, `priceChangePercent`). Open interest is per symbol only.
- Bitget USDT-FUTURES `tickers` (rate, `markPrice`, `holdingAmount`, `usdtVolume`, `change24h`)
  and `contracts` (`fundInterval`, `symbolType`, `symbolStatus`).
- Gate USDT `contracts` (rate, `funding_interval`, `funding_next_apply`, `mark_price`,
  `quanto_multiplier`, `in_delisting`, `status`) and `tickers` (`volume_24h_settle`,
  `total_size`, `change_percentage`).
- GRVT `all_instruments` and one `ticker` per instrument. The Python SDK documents
  `funding_rate_8h_curr` as "expressed in percentage points" and `open_interest` in base units.
  A 20-request burst returned 200 for every call in 0.12 s; the published limits page is behind
  a bot wall, so the adapter caps fan-out at sixteen.
- Variational `metadata/v2/stats` (documented public read-only API, 10 requests per 10 s).
  Its own `annualized_rate` for BTC-PERP (0.1095) equals simple annualization of
  `interval_rate` over `interval_seconds`, which confirms the convention.
- QFEX `md/contracts` (public per its OpenAPI). The funding page defines the rate as the
  premium in basis points divided by 100, so the value is a percentage per 60-minute window.

## Decisions

- Scope filters mirror the existing adapters: MEXC keeps USDT, USDC, and USD1 settlement
  (coin-margined contracts quote open interest in contract units); Bitget reads the USDT-M
  product only; Gate reads the USDT book and skips delisting contracts; Variational keeps
  perpetuals (swaps carry separate long and short rates and a daily interval); QFEX keeps
  USD quotes. Each skipped case is a recorded row with a test.
- GRVT quotes an eight-hour rate for every instrument, including four-hour ones. The adapter
  scales it to the instrument interval so the hourly and annualized rates are unchanged.
- QFEX publishes no mark price. The adapter leaves `markPrice` unset rather than substituting
  the index, so entry cost shows as unmeasured for those pairs.
- BingX open interest and Bitget next-funding time are per-symbol endpoints; both stay unknown
  rather than adding hundreds of requests per scan.
- Default taker fees are the venues' published base tiers: MEXC 2 (contract feed), BingX 5
  (contract list), Bitget 6 (contract list), Gate 7.5 (contract feed), GRVT 4.5 (public fee
  schedule), Variational 0 (no trading fee; the venue earns the spread), QFEX 10 (tier 5 single
  stocks; commodities and indices are 5). All are overridable with `taker_fee_bps.<venue>`.
- History (`mob history`) and market metadata are unchanged; the new venues are scan-only.

## Dependencies, questions and omissions

- Dependencies: none added.
- QFEX rate units follow its funding page (premium in basis points divided by 100, so a
  percentage per hour). The recorded nonzero samples are tiny either way; a live cross-check
  against a Hyperliquid `xyz:` stock perpetual on a volatile day would settle it.
- GRVT publishes no reachable rate-limit page; the adapter issues one ticker request per
  active instrument (184 today), sixteen at a time, and a 20-request burst was accepted.
- Not added: funding history for the seven venues (`mob history` still covers HL, Binance,
  Aster, Bybit, and OKX), Bitget USDC and coin products, Gate's BTC-settled book, MEXC
  coin-margined contracts, Variational swaps, and non-USD QFEX quotes.
- The scan now hides many more thin pairs by default (11,664 in the live run); the $1M per-leg
  volume filter is unchanged.
- The version was not bumped. Plugin users receive these venues at the next release.
